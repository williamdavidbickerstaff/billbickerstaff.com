I am an artist living and working in Cambridge, producing work that explores ideas around story and legend through a multidisciplinary practice including printmaking, drawing and sculpture, but also writing and digital mediums. My mark-making and intaglio work comes from a sketching practice with imagery always drawn from imagination, capturing the ideographic with essential, elemental forms. Digital projects are wide ranging with recent work exploring atypical forms such as game modding and creative ricing. Sometimes the work is less focused on itself as a visual medium, but instead on writing to explore ideas of communities and personas, obfuscation and shattered forms.
