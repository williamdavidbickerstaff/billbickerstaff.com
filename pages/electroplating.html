Electroplating

This project came largely from an interest in the process; one that is adjacent to intaglio, using similar masking methods, but instead working with an additive, not subtractive process. Instead of Ferric Chloride etching into the copper, I developed a process where a solution of Copper Sulphate (CuSO₄) uses electroplating to deposit copper onto the plate’s surface. The final piece was therefore not the print, but the plate itself. This was used to depict an elemental landscape where path, tree, forest, lake, mountain and comet become archetypes, conveying a sense of myth where the landscape itself are the characters.

Drawings

The preliminary sketches are drawn from imagination, and came about after a long process of drawing similar scenes repeatedly. After a while, I found ways of drawing the same elements (such as the lake cut in half) in ways which felt more than representative, like archetypes or icons. Used repeatedly in different scenes, these elements became their own characters in a universal inscape.

The trees in drawing seven are part of a forest, with its associations of threat and disorientation. However, as a group of three in front of a lake, they become protectors, shielding the viewer from the black water. The comet, turned upside down becomes a figure, with its light trail limbs. Even the pattern of dots became both the paths and the stars.

Process

It took a long time to work out the process. I knew that it was probably possible, as I had seen Matthew Barney do something similar at the Hayward Gallery in 2021. It was just a matter of trial and error to get it right, and it took me a couple of months to refine. The main variables were the exact solution to use, and the strength of the current. I developed a process where two plates of copper (cathode and anode) were put into a solution of CuSO₄ and C₆H₈O₇, then passed a current through the plates. The copper is taken from the anode, and deposited on the cathode (on the areas of the plate that had not been masked with asphalt ground).
