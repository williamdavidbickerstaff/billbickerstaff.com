***Writing***

***Hamlet***

‘Hamlet’ is in the form of a network of pages held together by a series of weblinks. It begins at the Beach, suggesting a location, a landscape, a physicality, but then defines no strict direction or timeline for you to follow. The site becomes an exploration, something to be curious about, where through the clicks of a mouse more and more is uncovered. It lies about itself, setting itself up as a piece of writing made by a network of people, users, when in fact they are all personas, pseudonyms belonging to me. ‘Beach’ plays with the idea of narrative, using a ubiquitous form almost beneath consideration. It obfuscates the author, shattering my own form, and sets up walls between the reader and the object by never explicitly stating the form of the subject.
